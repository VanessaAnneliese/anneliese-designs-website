# Anneliese Designs brand guide

Follow these rules for every design, copy and code change. Full design system with live previews: "Anneliese Designs" design system in Claude.

## Business facts
- Owner: Vanessa Witkowski, Anneliese Designs, Whitby, Durham Region, Ontario
- Owner of Anneliese Designs since 2006; designing since 1990. Use the years, not a count of years.
- Services, presented as separate, equal offers: Brand Identity, Graphic Design, Web Design, Strategic Writing, Content Strategy, Information Architecture, Social Media Design, AI-assisted web workflows, AI-visibility tracking. AI-visibility tracking is its own service, never part of web design.
- Core value: accessible pricing without cutting corners on quality.
- Tagline: "Creating the world you dream!" CTA: "Ready to bring your vision to life?" / "Let's Work Together".

## Voice
- Warm, joyful, personal. First person singular: "I" to one client, "you". Never "we" or "our team".
- Never use long dashes (em or en dashes), and never a spaced hyphen as a dash (" - "). Use a comma, colon, full stop or parentheses.
- Sentence case for headings. No emoji in body copy. Exclamation marks sparingly.

## Type
- **Cambo** (Georgia, serif fallback): headings, body, buttons. Cambo exists ONLY at weight 400. Never set it bold or 500/700; build hierarchy with size and spacing.
- **Cormorant Garamond**: only the ANNELIESE DESIGNS wordmark (23px, weight 300, uppercase, 0.38em tracking; 500 in the closing block; 0.25em on legal pages) and the italic tagline (weight 300).
- **Lato**: footer only (headings 14px 700 uppercase 0.1em tracking; text 14px; small 12px).
- Arvo is loaded but unused; remove it. Do not add other fonts.
- Sizes: legal H1 30/36; section H2 20/28; legal H2 18/28; hero lead 18px, 1.625; body 16px, 1.625 (legal bodies 1.8); button 16px uppercase, 0.025em; small 14/20.

## Layout
- One centred column, max width 672px (max-w-2xl), 24px side padding, centred text on home.
- Sections 40px top and bottom; hero, closing block and footer 48px; legal main 56px.
- Homepage order: header (90px logo, then wordmark), lead paragraph, alternating illustration dividers (325px) and content sections, closing logo block (100px logo, wordmark, tagline), footer.
- Corners square except the CTA (8px) and social circles (full). No shadows.
- Focus: 2px solid red outline, 2px offset (white outline in the footer).

## Imagery
- Illustration dividers: vivid rainbow paint-splash illustrations, single subject on pure white. New ones must match. No stock photos.
- Order: icon-1, icon-2, icon-3, pencil, banner, icon-5. icon-4 is unused (good for an About or AI-visibility section).
- Logo: public/images/logo.png. Never recolour, stretch, add effects, or place on red.
